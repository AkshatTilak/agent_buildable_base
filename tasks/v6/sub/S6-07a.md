## Scope

Break the hard-coded assumption that an eval suite belongs to exactly one agent. In V5,
`eval_test_suites.agent_id` and `eval_run_history.agent_id` are non-null foreign keys into
`agent_definitions` — a workflow can never be a target. V6 replaces them with a polymorphic triple
(`target_type`, `target_hub_id`, `target_id`) and adds `hub_id` to `eval_test_suites`,
`eval_run_history` and `eval_flow_traces` so eval data is tenant-isolated like everything else.

`eval_test_cases` also gains the columns needed for node-level assertions, so a test case can assert on
an intermediate workflow node rather than only on the final answer. This subtask ships the models,
schemas and reversible Alembic revision; the runner changes are S6-07b/c.
